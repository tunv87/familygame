No login or account is needed.

Try on one device:
- Home → "Tìm số / Find the Number" → "Chơi với máy / Play vs computer" → Easy.
- Home → "Cờ ca rô / Gomoku" → "Chơi với máy / Play vs computer" → Small board → Easy.
- Home → "Cờ gánh / Co Ganh" → "Chơi với máy / Play vs computer" → Easy.
- Practice mode and two players on one device also work offline.

Online play ("Chơi với bạn / Play with a friend") needs a second device: create a room on one device and enter the 4-digit code on the other. The second player can also join from a web browser at https://tunv87.github.io/familygame/ ("Join a room").

The app language follows the device (Vietnamese or English) and can be changed in Settings.
