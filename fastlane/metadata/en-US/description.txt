Family Games – five games with big, clear buttons so a 7-year-old and a 70-year-old can play together.

🔢 FIND THE NUMBER
Numbers 1 to 30, 60 or 100 are scattered across the board. Whoever taps the next number first gets it!
• Play the robot: Easy, Medium and Hard
• Practice: race the clock and beat your own record
• Play a friend: each on your own device, on the same board

⭕ GOMOKU (TIC-TAC-TOE & FIVE IN A ROW)
• Small 3×3 board for little ones: three in a row wins
• Big 15×15 board for grown-ups: five in a row wins
• Play the robot (3 levels), two players on one device, or a faraway friend

🪨 Ô ĂN QUAN – A VIETNAMESE MANCALA GAME
Five rice fields on each side and a mandarin square at each end. Pick up the stones, choose a direction, sow – and capture after an empty square!
• Keep sowing, capture again and again, re-seed when you run out – the app counts the points
• Play the robot (Easy, Medium, Hard), two players on one device, or a faraway friend

⚫ CỜ GÁNH – A VIETNAMESE FOLK GAME
5×5 board, 8 pieces each. Captured pieces change colour instead of leaving the board!
• Gánh (carry): move between two enemy pieces in a line → both become yours
• Surround: enemy pieces that cannot move become yours
• Mở (open): if your opponent leaves a gap you can capture into, you must capture – the app highlights the forced move
• Play the robot (Easy, Medium, Hard), two players on one device, or a faraway friend

🔗 WORD CHAIN – A VIETNAMESE WORD GAME
"học sinh" → "sinh viên" → "viên phấn"… Whoever cannot continue the chain loses!
• Just type the second syllable – with the right tone marks, good spelling practice
• Every word shows its English meaning: great for children learning Vietnamese abroad
• A dictionary of over 17,000 words, filtered for children
• Play the robot (Easy, Medium, Hard), two players on one device, or a faraway friend

📖 EVERY GAME HAS A STORY
Each game has step-by-step rules and a short story about where it comes from and what its name means – to share with the little ones.
