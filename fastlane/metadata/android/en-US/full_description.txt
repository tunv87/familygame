Family Games – two timeless games with big, clear buttons so a 7-year-old and a 70-year-old can play together.

🔢 FIND THE NUMBER
Numbers 1 to 30, 60 or 100 are scattered across the board. Whoever taps the next number first gets it!
• Play the robot: Easy, Medium and Hard
• Practice: race the clock and beat your own record
• Play a friend: each on your own device, on the same board

⭕ GOMOKU (TIC-TAC-TOE & FIVE IN A ROW)
• Small 3×3 board for little ones: three in a row wins
• Big 15×15 board for grown-ups: five in a row wins
• Play the robot (3 levels), two players on one device, or a faraway friend

👫 PLAYING WITH FRIENDS IS EASY
Create a room and share the 4-digit code. Friends without the app can still join from a web browser.
